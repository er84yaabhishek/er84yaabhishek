#### 🏨 1. Enterprise Hotel Management System (HMS)
- **High-Concurrency Reservation Engine:** Real-time room inventory management, tariff rate approval matrices, and guest folio balancing.
- **Asynchronous Guest Communication:** Background queue workers handling confirmation, amendment, and cancellation letters with instant PDF attachments.
- **Point of Sale (POS) Integration:** Multi-outlet billing, Kitchen Order Tickets (KOT), and integrated guest ledger settlements.

#### 🏢 2. Commercial Real Estate & Mall Management System (MMS)
- **Multi-Tenant Architecture:** Scalable Database-per-Property architecture with runtime connection switching and automated migration pipelines.
- **Automatic & Manual Payment Reminder Engine:** Intelligent daily cron scanner that calculates lease due dates, computes live outstanding balance (`Bill + Debit Note - Credit Note - Receipts`), halts instantly when balance hits zero, and prevents duplicate mails.
- **Payment Reminder Console:** Real-time UI for accountants with live KPI tracking, single-click manual triggers, bulk dispatches, and complete timeline audit history.
- **Financial FIFO Allocation Engine:** Reconciles multi-bill invoices with partial payments and credit notes in chronological order.
- **Utility & Power Calculation:** Automated Grid & DG power consumption tracking with slab-based escalations and facility bill aggregation.
